# MOCHI Platform Internal Training Presentation Design

## 1. Overview
- **Purpose**: Internal employee training and onboarding for the GrapeSEED MOCHI platform.
- **Audience**: All internal teams (CO, CS, Coach) and Administrators (Full Access).
- **Format**: PowerPoint Presentation (via Figma Slides)
- **Length**: ~23 slides
- **Structure**: Lifecycle-driven (from lead discovery to admin setup). This helps employees understand how data flows through the system and how different teams collaborate.

## 2. Design & Visual Strategy
- **Tone**: Professional, clear, and instructive.
- **Visuals**: Use screenshots of the actual platform where applicable to ground the learning in reality. 
- **Navigation Cues**: Each section should be clearly delimited. Since it's lifecycle-based, consider a progress bar or timeline indicator across the slides.

## 3. Slide Outline & Content Structure

### Part 1: Introduction & Initial Setup
**Section 1: Introduction**
- **Slide 1**: Title (GrapeSEED MOCHI Platform User Guide)
- **Slide 2**: What is MOCHI? (Purpose, core values, centralizing operations)
- **Slide 3**: Getting Started (Login requirements, profile management, checking notifications)
- **Slide 4**: Understanding Permissions (General User vs. Full Access vs. GS Brochure Admin)

**Section 2: Discovery & Contract (Lead Management)**
- **Slide 5**: Managing Potential Institutions (CO Team focus, lead registration)
- **Slide 6**: Contract Conversion Process (How leads transition to the main institution list)

**Section 3: Setup & Contacts**
- **Slide 7**: Using the Institution List (Searching, verifying info, assigning managers)
- **Slide 8**: Managing School Contacts (Registering staff, updating status, contact details)

### Part 2: Operations & Collaboration
**Section 4: Support & Scheduling**
- **Slide 9**: Writing Support Reports (Logging visits and tasks for regular/potential institutions)
- **Slide 10**: Institution Support History (Viewing past support, uploading/managing contracts)
- **Slide 11**: Schedule Management (Using the calendar, adding personal/team events)
- **Slide 12**: Schedule Visibility (Private vs. Team Public events, viewing colleagues' schedules)

**Section 5: Logistics & Assets**
- **Slide 13**: Store Inventory Management (Viewing stock, making adjustments with reasons)
- **Slide 14**: Store Sales History (Tracking deductions and sales)
- **Slide 15**: Salesforce Files (Connecting and managing Salesforce documents)
- **Slide 16**: GS Brochure - Request Process (External application flow and internal verification)
- **Slide 17**: GS Brochure - Admin Dashboard (Inventory, waybills, and logistics overview)

### Part 3: Administration & Wrap-up
**Section 6: Administration (Full Access Only)**
- **Slide 18**: People Management (Viewing the employee master list, department transfers)
- **Slide 19**: System Setup (Creating teams, managing common codes/statuses)
- **Slide 20**: User Registration & Roles (Creating new accounts, assigning permissions)

**Section 7: Conclusion & FAQ**
- **Slide 21**: Troubleshooting FAQ Part 1 (Login failures, missing menus, missing edit buttons)
- **Slide 22**: Troubleshooting FAQ Part 2 (Visibility issues: leads not showing, colleague schedules hidden)
- **Slide 23**: Resources & Help (Links to GS Brochure Policy, Profile Email Policy, Helpdesk contact)

## 4. Implementation Notes (Figma Slides)
- **Template**: The presentation will be built using a new Figma Slides file.
- **Theme**: Will establish a clean, corporate theme suitable for internal training (likely utilizing GrapeSEED brand colors if available, or a clean modern default).
- **Structure**: Each "Section" above will map to a `SLIDE_ROW` in the Figma Slides grid to organize the presentation logically for the presenter.
- **Speaker Notes**: Will generate concise speaker notes for each slide to assist the presenter during training sessions.
